---
name: Market Research Brief
description: Evidence-first research lean for a ticker, with uncertainty
---

1. Use only fetched evidence (price bars, filings, macro, news). Quote its as-of date.
2. If live data is unavailable or older than the freshness limit, output status unavailable or stale and stop. Never fill gaps from memory.
3. State bullish, base and bearish cases with the evidence for each, then what would invalidate the lean.
4. A lean is research support. Never phrase it as an instruction to buy, sell or hold, and never mention order sizes or prices to trade at.
5. Report specialist disagreement as disagreement. Do not average it into a single confident answer.
